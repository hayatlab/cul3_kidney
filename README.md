# cul3_kidney
Running title: Cul3 KO impact on Kidney

Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is characterized by proximal tubule (PT) stress, oxidative injury, and metabolic dysfunction. Analysis of a human kidney single-nucleus RNA-sequencing (snRNA-seq) dataset identified proteasome-mediated ubiquitin-dependent protein catabolic processes among the most enriched pathways in injured PT cells, suggesting activation of the ubiquitin-proteasome system during tubular stress. Because Cullin 3 (CUL3), a core scaffold of Cullin-RING E3 ubiquitin ligases, regulates oxidative stress signaling through the KEAP1-NRF2 axis, we investigated its role in PT injury and stress adaptation.
